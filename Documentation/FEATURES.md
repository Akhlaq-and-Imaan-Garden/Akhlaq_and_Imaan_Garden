# ✨ Ramadan Quests - Complete Features List

## 🎯 **Core Features (All Implemented)**

### 📅 **Daily Quest System**
- ✅ 30 unique Islamic quests for kids aged 6-9
- ✅ Auto-unlock based on current Ramadan day
- ✅ Quest categories: Faith & Worship, Quran Learning, Good Deeds & Character, Creative Activities, Family Bonding

- ✅ Visual status indicators (completed, locked, current, available)

- ✅ Automatic badge awarding

- ✅ Earned vs locked badge states

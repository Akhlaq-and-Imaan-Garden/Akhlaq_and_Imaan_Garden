About Akhlaq and Imaan Garden

About : Akhlaq and Imaan Garden is a small Ramadan companion app designed to help children (around ages 6–9) build good character and strengthen their connection with Allah through simple daily quests.

How It Works : Each day, your child will see one gentle challenge (for example: reading a short surah, helping at home, or doing an act of kindness) and can mark it as completed to see their progress grow over the 30 days of Ramadan.

Privacy : We store only the minimum information needed to track quests and badges. You can clear all local data anytime from the Parent section.
